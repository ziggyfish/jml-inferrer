\section{Empirical Measurement}
\label{sec:empirical}

\subsection{Subjects and method}

We measure on the source jars of Apache Commons Lang~3.14.0 (246
source files, 3{,}494 methods produce a non-empty isolated spec) and
Apache Commons IO~2.13.0 (245 source files, 2{,}111 methods produce a
non-empty isolated spec). These are the same libraries used by the
predecessor work~\cite{edmonds2026inference, edmonds2026embedding};
using them lets cross-paper comparisons proceed without confounding
from corpus differences. The Guava library, which the predecessor
papers also cover, is excluded from the present measurement: its
source-jar size pushes the JavaParser-driven harness into a deep
recursion that overflows the default JVM stack
(Section~\ref{sec:threats}).

For each library, the harness:

\begin{enumerate}\setlength\itemsep{0.2em}
  \item Parses every \texttt{.java} entry in the sources jar.
  \item Builds the call graph
        (\texttt{CallGraphBuilder.buildFromCompilationUnits}).
  \item Runs the existing
        \texttt{MethodSpecificationInferrer.inferSpecification} on
        every method declaration. The inferrer's internal
        \texttt{InterproceduralAnalyzer} runs as part of this pass,
        propagating callee preconditions in the standard single-pass
        manner. The resulting per-method precondition and
        postcondition counts are snapshotted as the
        \emph{isolated baseline}.
  \item Invokes
        \texttt{CompositionalAnalyzer.refineAll()} on the populated
        cache, which mutates each method's spec in place.
  \item For each method, diffs the post-refinement spec against the
        baseline and counts newly-added clauses. Each new
        precondition is categorised by syntactic shape (null check,
        range/bound, branch-conditional implication, disjunctive,
        other) for the RQ2 distribution.
\end{enumerate}

All measurements are deterministic given the inputs; we report each
exactly once. The harness, the per-library output, and the
configuration knobs are committed to the replication package.

\textbf{RQ1 is answered: the second pass strictly extends the
isolated set on both libraries.} On Commons Lang, 43.85\% of methods
in the cache receive at least one new clause from the refinement; on
Commons IO, 36.33\% do. In total the pass adds 7{,}764 new
precondition clauses and 7{,}221 new postcondition clauses across
the two libraries. The single-pass propagation is \emph{not}
subsuming. The result holds even though Commons Lang's
\texttt{InterproceduralAnalyzer} pass was already doing single-pass
callee-precondition propagation during isolated inference; the
second pass discovers obligations the first pass cannot express.

\subsection{Shape distribution}

Table~\ref{tab:shapes} categorises the newly-added preconditions by
syntactic shape. The categorisation runs on each new clause
independently and is structurally exhaustive (every clause falls in
exactly one bucket; ties resolve in favour of structural shape
over content, so a clause containing \texttt{==>} is counted as
branch-conditional even if it also mentions \texttt{!= null}).

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\caption{Syntactic shape of the newly-added preconditions. Branch-conditional implications dominate on both libraries, reflecting that the second pass walks the caller's control-flow context that the single pass does not.}
\label{tab:shapes}
\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}
\toprule
& \multicolumn{2}{c}{\textbf{Commons Lang}} & \multicolumn{2}{c}{\textbf{Commons IO}} \\
\cmidrule(lr){2-3}\cmidrule(lr){4-5}
\textbf{Shape} & count & \% & count & \% \\
\midrule
Branch-conditional (\texttt{==>}) & 3{,}029 & 52.6\% & 857 & 42.8\% \\
Null check (\texttt{!= null} / \texttt{== null}) & 1{,}255 & 21.8\% & 695 & 34.7\% \\
Range / bound (\texttt{<}, \texttt{<=}, \texttt{>}, \texttt{>=}) & 1{,}143 & 19.8\% & 378 & 18.9\% \\
Disjunctive (\texttt{||}) & 248 & 4.3\% & 36 & 1.8\% \\

\midrule

\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\textbf{RQ2 is answered: branch-conditional implications dominate, and
they are the shape the single pass structurally cannot produce.} On
Commons Lang, 52.6\% of all newly-added preconditions are
\texttt{guard ==> obligation} clauses; on Commons IO, 42.8\%. These
clauses arise from calls inside an \texttt{if} or ternary branch
whose enclosing guard must be lifted into the precondition. A
single-pass propagation, which appends the callee's substituted
preconditions \emph{unconditionally}, can produce
the \texttt{obligation} part but cannot synthesise the \texttt{guard ==> }
prefix because it has no access to the caller's control-flow
context at the point of propagation.

Null checks (22--35\%) and range/bound clauses (19\%) make up most
of the remainder. These are clauses the single pass \emph{would}
produce when the call site is unguarded; the second pass adds them
when the call site is guarded by a condition the single pass would
not lift. Disjunctive clauses (1.8--4.3\%) come from polymorphic
dispatch sites where multiple subtype callees contribute alternative
obligations; the residual \emph{other} (1.5--1.7\%) is the long-tail
shape that includes arithmetic comparisons not matched by the
range/bound regex.

\subsection{Runtime cost}

The second pass completes in 3.5 seconds on Commons Lang
(3{,}494 methods, 6{,}223 SCCs visited, all singletons) and
proportionally on Commons IO (2{,}111 methods, 9 seconds for both
runs combined including parsing and the baseline isolated pass).
No SCC hit the fixpoint cap; the call graphs of both libraries are
acyclic under the analyser's signature-matching scheme. The
refinement step is therefore an addition of seconds on top of an
inference pass that already takes minutes on libraries of this
size; it is a clearly affordable enhancement.

\textbf{RQ3 is answered: the second pass is cheap and fixpoint
iteration never fires at the libraries' scale.} The runtime
characteristic is dominated by the AST walk per method
(\texttt{decl.findAll(MethodCallExpr.class)}), which is linear in
the body size; the lifting and substitution steps add constant
factors per call site. The cost scales with the number of call
sites in the corpus (7{,}719 on Commons Lang, 4{,}213 on
Commons IO).
